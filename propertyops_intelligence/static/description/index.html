PropertyOps Intelligence

Lease Administration, Tenant Portal & Portfolio Control

Odoo 18 AI-Powered Commercial & Residential

PropertyOps Intelligence is a complete property management module for Odoo 18 that streamlines lease administration, tenant lifecycle, rent scheduling, maintenance operations, vendor compliance, and move-in/move-out workflows across your real estate portfolio. Every core record carries predictive AI fields so portfolio managers can act on signals rather than hindsight.

Core Modules

Properties

Track buildings, property types, square footage, ownership and AI occupancy forecasts at the portfolio level.

Units & Suites

Manage rentable units with status tracking, market rent estimates and turnover dates.

Tenants

Maintain tenant master data with credit scores, churn-risk scoring and full lease history.

Leases

Full lease lifecycle with escalation types (fixed, CPI, step), renewal options, CAM charges and AI renewal probability.

Rent Schedule

Automated rent schedule entries with overdue tracking, partial payments and days-late calculations.

Maintenance Requests

Tenant-submittable work orders with AI urgency scoring, vendor suggestions and triage workflow.

Vendor Compliance

Insurance and policy tracking with compliance scoring and expiring/expired alerts.

Move In / Out

Inspection checklists, deposit handling, deductions and unit readiness tracking with vacancy-day metrics.

AI-Driven Insights

Occupancy Forecast

Predictive occupancy percentage per property to anticipate vacancies and leasing velocity.

Churn Risk

Tenant-level churn-risk scoring to surface at-risk accounts before renewal deadlines.

Renewal Probability

Lease-level renewal probability so managers can prioritize outreach and negotiation.

Market Rent Estimate

Unit-level market rent estimates to benchmark asking rents against current market conditions.

Urgency Score

Maintenance request urgency scoring with AI-suggested vendors for faster dispatch.
